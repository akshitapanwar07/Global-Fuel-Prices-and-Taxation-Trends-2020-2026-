# 🌍 Global Fuel Prices and Taxation Trends (2020–2026)

An interactive data visualization project that explores global fuel prices, taxation patterns, and country-wise and regional comparisons of petrol, diesel, LPG, and Brent crude oil.

The project uses **Tableau Public** to present fuel price trends and taxation data through interactive visualizations, helping users explore differences across countries and regions.

## 📊 Project Overview

Fuel prices vary across countries and regions due to differences in taxation, market conditions, and other economic factors. Understanding these variations can be challenging when data is presented only in tables.

This project provides an interactive dashboard for exploring fuel prices and taxation trends from **2020 to 2026**, making it easier to compare fuel types, investigate regional differences, and understand patterns in the available data.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Petrol Price Comparison:** Compare petrol prices across different countries.
* **Diesel Price Comparison:** Explore country-wise diesel price variations.
* **LPG Price Analysis:** Investigate LPG prices across countries and regions.
* **Brent Crude Oil Analysis:** Explore crude oil price trends and their relationship with fuel prices.
* **Fuel Tax Analysis:** Compare fuel tax percentages across countries.
* **Regional Tax Analysis:** Examine differences in taxation patterns across geographical regions.
* **Interactive Visualizations:** Explore charts and compare available fuel price and tax data.
* **Historical Trend Analysis:** Investigate patterns across the 2020–2026 project period.

## ⚠️ Data and Interpretation Notes

* Findings depend on the dataset's coverage, definitions, and units.
* Regional averages may conceal differences between individual countries.
* Observed relationships between crude oil prices, retail fuel prices, and taxes do not independently establish causation.
* Confirm the data source and latest update date before using the results for policy or financial decisions.
